# Changelog

## [1.0.0]

- First release: disciplines raced against your own ghost (record holder, last time, the standard, a month ago), on a Munich-1972 track with pictograms after Otl Aicher (33 of them).
- Sprint, endurance and pace disciplines; splits with live deltas, best sections and the perfect heat; pit stops with reasons.
- Qualifying, then gold, silver and bronze for your top three; records; rushed heats disqualified.
- The Games every four weeks: a medal table by part of life, Games records, the closing ceremony and the flame.
- Entries before the start, conditions, the coach's notes, the learning curve, relays, the photo finish, heats by hand.
- A live clock in the notification with split, pit-stop and finish buttons; a quick-settings tile and launcher shortcuts; backup to one file. English and Russian.
